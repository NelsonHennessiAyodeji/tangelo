### **Slide 2: The Problem**

**The Nigerian Wedding: A Cultural Celebration, A Logistical Nightmare.**

Planning a Nigerian wedding is a beautiful, culturally rich experience, but it's notoriously complex and stressful.

*   **Fragmented Planning:** Couples juggle spreadsheets, notebooks, and multiple chat apps. There's no single source of truth.
*   **Cultural Nuances:** Planning requires deep knowledge of traditions (e.g., Aso Ebi, Traditional Wedding vs. White Wedding) that generic tools miss.
*   **Budget Overruns:** With multiple events and high guest counts, managing finances is a massive challenge, often leading to significant overspending.
*   **Vendor Discovery:** Finding trusted, quality vendors that fit a specific style and budget in a crowded market is difficult and time-consuming.

---

### **Slide 3: The Solution**

**Introducing Tangelo: Your AI Wedding Planner.**

Tangelo is an all-in-one, mobile-first platform that simplifies every aspect of planning a Nigerian wedding.

We combine intuitive planning tools with the power of AI to provide a personalized, stress-free experience from engagement to honeymoon.

**We are the digital *Aso Ebi* — bringing everything and everyone together, beautifully.**

### **Slide 4: Key Features (The "How")**

**(Showcase screenshots from the demo app for each feature.)**

*   **Multi-Event Dashboard:** A personalized hub that provides a high-level overview of all wedding events, from the Traditional Ceremony to the Reception, including a countdown to the big day.

*   **Smart, Per-Event Budgeting:** Users set individual budgets for each event. Our tool provides a visual breakdown of expenses and tracks spending to prevent costly surprises.

*   **Interactive, AI-Powered Checklist:** A culturally-aware checklist suggests tasks based on event type (e.g., "Arrange Aso Ebi"). AI helps generate tailored task lists, ensuring nothing is missed.

*   **Seamless Guest Management:** Easily import guest lists via CSV, track RSVPs for each event, manage dietary needs, and plan seating arrangements with a simple drag-and-drop interface.

*   **AI Vendor Concierge & Marketplace:** Users can browse a curated vendor marketplace or use our AI assistant. Simply describe your needs, and Tangelo finds and suggests the perfect local photographers, caterers, and venues that match your style and budget.

*   **AI Moodboard Generator:** Couples describe their dream wedding, and our AI instantly generates a stunning visual moodboard, turning abstract ideas into a concrete vision.

### **Slide 5: The Market Opportunity**

**A Large, Underserved, and Highly Motivated Market.**

*   **Market Size:** The Nigerian wedding industry is a multi-billion dollar market, with the average wedding costing millions of Naira.
*   **Target Audience:** Tech-savvy millennials and Gen Z in Nigeria and the diaspora who expect modern, digital solutions.
*   **The Gap:** Current wedding planning tools are generic and fail to address the specific cultural and logistical needs of the Nigerian market. Tangelo is built from the ground up to fill this gap.
*   **High Willingness to Spend:** Couples and their families invest heavily to create a memorable, successful wedding celebration.

### **Slide 6: Business Model**

**Freemium Model with a Clear Path to Monetization.**

*   **Free Tier (Our Current Demo):**
    *   Core planning tools for up to 2 events.
    *   Full access to budgeting, guest list, and checklist features.
    *   This builds our user base and gathers valuable data.

*   **Premium Tier (Future Growth):**
    *   **Unlimited Events:** For complex, multi-day celebrations.
    *   **Vendor Commission:** A percentage fee for bookings made through the platform.
    *   **Advanced AI:** Deeper vendor analysis, contract review suggestions, and automated timeline generation.
    *   **Financial Services:** Integrations for wedding loans or payment processing.
    *   **Marketplace for Wedding Items:** Aso Ebi, souvenirs, etc.
